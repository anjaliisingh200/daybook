# TaskCard
Source: `TaskCard.tsx`

One task in the Tasks view.

- Circle completion check on the left, then the title (medium weight, no description).
- Bottom row: date chip (overdue in destructive colour) and priority.
- No border, no shadow, no radius, 2px padding.
- Hover: grey background.
- Compact variant (week columns) is the same card, narrower.
- Drag a card onto another day to change its date.
